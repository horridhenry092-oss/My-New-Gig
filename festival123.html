Annual Cycle

The Rhythm of Indian Seasons

Indian festivals follow ancient lunar-solar calendars, aligning human joy with harvest cycles and changing skies.

Winter • Jan – Feb

Sun & Harvest

Marked by Pongal and Makar Sankranti, thanking the Sun God for abundant winter crops with sweet rice and kite-filled skies.

Spring • Mar – Apr

Blossoms & Colors

Holi heralds spring’s arrival with clouds of gulal, folk songs, and renewal, alongside springtime crescent moon celebrations.

Monsoon • Aug – Sep

Emerald Abundance

Lush rains usher in Onam flower carpets in Kerala and the rhythmic drums of Ganesh Chaturthi across Maharashtra.

Autumn • Oct – Nov

Triumph & Radiance

The grand crescendo of Navratri, Durga Puja, and Diwali illuminates cities with artistry, dance, and millions of lamps.

Shared Heritage

Where Art, Devotion & Hospitality Meet

Whether drawing intricate Kolam and Pookalam patterns at dawn, sharing warm bowls of Sheer Khurma and Payasam with neighbors, or lighting clay Diyas along courtyards, every Indian festival transforms everyday spaces into living works of art.
